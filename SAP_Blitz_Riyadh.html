Ground Rules

The Rules

Understand these before the sprint begins. No exceptions on blitz day.

1
Net New Accounts Only
Accounts must have zero prior SAP engagement. No existing customers, no active opportunities, no install base.
2
Pre-Approved List Required
Only accounts from your SAP-approved list count toward the leaderboard. Off-list accounts will be disqualified.
3
Account Exclusivity — First Submission Wins
Account lists are confidential. In the event of overlap, the partner who submitted first retains the account.
4
Leaderboard Shows Numbers Only
Live standings track leads qualified. No account names are ever visible to competing partners.
5
Hard Submission Deadline
All leads must be submitted before the hard stop. Late submissions will not count toward the leaderboard.
Qualification Standard
All Three Criteria Must Be Met
C
Executive Contact Reached
C-suite, VP, or Director — a decision-maker or strong influencer in the buying process.
8C
At Least One 8C Criterion Confirmed
Compelling Event, Funding, Stakeholder Strategy, Challenges, Value Drivers, Solution, Competitors, or Ecosystem.
N
Follow-up Action Agreed
Discovery call booked, demo scheduled, or proposal requested on the call.
The 8 Deal Qualification Criteria
1
Compelling Event
Why is the customer looking for a new solution, and why right now?
2
Funding
Has funding been secured? Where does it come from and what are the constraints?
3
Customer Stakeholder Strategy
Who is impacted and who makes or influences decisions?
4
Customer Challenges
What are they struggling with?
5
Business Value Drivers
How does each stakeholder measure success?
6
Solution & Differentiators
Why is SAP the best choice for this customer project?
7
Competitors
Who are we competing against and how strong are we in comparison?
8
Ecosystem
Which partners are already involved? Which should be?
Leads not meeting all three qualification criteria will be reviewed by SAP and will not count toward the leaderboard.
